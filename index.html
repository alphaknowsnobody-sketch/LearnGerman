So… what is this?

A speaking club where the goal is to suck loudly and laugh about it.

You've taken the A1 course. You've watched the YouTube videos. You've downloaded three apps. And then someone says "Hallo, wie geht's?" at the bakery and your brain just… leaves the building.

SprichMit?! is the missing piece — a low-stakes space to actually open your mouth and use what you know, with people who get it because they're in the same boat. Native speakers come too. Snacks are involved.

The vibe, in 5 lines

  • ✓ Real conversations over coffee
  • ✓ Mistakes are the curriculum
  • ✓ Mixed levels — beginners welcome
  • ✕ No textbooks, no homework
  • ✕ No "now repeat after me"

A taste of the cheat sheet

Five phrases that'll save you on Saturday.

When you arrive
Hallo, ich heiße…
Hi, my name is…
When you're lost
Ich verstehe nicht.
I don't understand.

Können Sie das wiederholen?

Why bother?

Speaking is the part nobody practices — and the part that actually matters.

01

Apps can't laugh with you

Duolingo won't react when you accidentally call your professor a sausage. Real people will, and you'll never forget the word again.

02

Network while you learn

Internships, flatmates, study groups, weekend plans — half of life in Germany happens in conversations you didn't plan to have.

03

Free, student-built

Made by students, for students, under Enactus Aachen. No course fees, no upsells — just show up.

Who's behind this?

A small team trying to fix a real problem.

SprichMit?! started with a familiar frustration: thousands of international students learn German on paper but never get fluent because they don't have anywhere safe to practice it out loud.

We're building it as part of Enactus — a global network of students using entrepreneurship to solve community problems. This first event is a test. If people show up and have fun, we scale it: regular sessions, level groups, partnerships with cafés and unis across Aachen and beyond.

So yeah — come help us prove the idea. ✌️
